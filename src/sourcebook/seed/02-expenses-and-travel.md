# Expenses and Travel

This guide covers what Northwind Labs pays for when you travel for work and how to get reimbursed.

## Booking travel

Book flights and hotels through the Navan travel portal. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Business class is allowed for flights longer than six hours.

## Meals and per diem

The daily meal allowance while travelling is $75 per day. The allowance covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except at team events approved by a director.

## Hotels

Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in most cities. In high-cost cities such as London, New York, San Francisco and Zurich the cap is $320 per night.

## Submitting expenses

Submit expenses in Ramp within 30 days of the purchase. Attach a receipt for every expense over $25. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run.

## Equipment purchases

Purchases above $500 need written approval from your manager before you buy. Software subscriptions must be requested through the IT portal instead of expensed.
