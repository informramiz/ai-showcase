# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Labs is a remote-first company. This policy explains where and when you can work, and what we expect in return.

## Working from home

Employees can work remotely up to four days a week. One day a week is spent at a hub office or a team offsite, chosen by each team. Fully remote contracts are available for roles approved by a department head.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 in your team's home time zone. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like. Please keep your calendar up to date so teammates know when you are available.

## Home office budget

New employees receive a one-time home office budget of $1,200. The budget covers a desk, a chair, a monitor and accessories. Each employee also receives an internet stipend of $45 per month, paid with salary.

## Working abroad

You can work from another country for up to 30 days in any 12-month period. Tell your manager and People Ops at least two weeks before you travel. Longer stays need approval from People Ops because of tax and visa rules.
