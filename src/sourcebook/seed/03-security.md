# Security Basics

Everyone at Northwind Labs helps keep customer data safe. These are the rules that apply to every employee and contractor.

## Accounts and passwords

Use the company password manager, 1Password, for every work account. Turn on two-factor authentication everywhere it is offered, with a hardware key for Google Workspace and GitHub. Never share passwords over chat or email.

## Devices

Company laptops must have disk encryption turned on and must install updates within 7 days of release. Lock your screen whenever you step away from your laptop.

## Lost or stolen devices

If your laptop or phone is lost or stolen, report it to the security team within one hour. Email security@northwindlabs.example or post in the #security-help Slack channel. The security team will lock and wipe the device remotely.

## Phishing

Forward suspicious emails to phishing@northwindlabs.example and then delete them. Do not click links or open attachments in a message you did not expect.
