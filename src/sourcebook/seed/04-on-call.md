# On-call Handbook

Engineers keep our services healthy around the clock with a shared on-call rotation.

## The rotation

Each on-call shift lasts one week, from Monday 10:00 to the following Monday 10:00. Every engineer joins the rotation after their first three months. Swaps are fine as long as you update PagerDuty.

## Response times

Acknowledge a page within 15 minutes. For a SEV1 incident, open an incident channel and post a first status update within 30 minutes.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a flat stipend of $400 for each week on call. Every hour spent handling an incident outside working hours is paid at $60 per hour. Engineers also get one day off after any night with more than two hours of incident work.

## Escalation

If you cannot resolve an incident within an hour, escalate to the secondary on-call engineer. The engineering manager on duty is the final escalation point.
